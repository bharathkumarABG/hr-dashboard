Senior managers — Level 5 and above — span, retention, absconding & early-tenure signals (shared filter)
Large spans are normal here — a senior manager overseeing several other managers is expected, not a concern by itself. Retention Risk is forward-looking — % of this manager's current team currently Serving Notice Period, i.e. people who've already resigned but haven't left yet. Voluntary attrition is historical — people who already left. These are deliberately separate signals.
Junior-level managers — Level 1 to 4 — span, retention, absconding & early-tenure signals
A junior-level manager carrying a large team is a different signal than a senior one — worth asking whether this is a genuine org structure choice or a data entry issue. Bringing retention and absconding in alongside span shows whether a manager's real problem is overload, retention, or both. Uses the same department filter above.

Manager assignment is each person's recorded Reporting Manager — for people who've already exited, that reflects their manager at the time, not necessarily today's org chart. Department filter uses each manager's own recorded department. This rate is never annualised or extrapolated — it's real exits that year ÷ that team's real average headcount that year. For the current year specifically, that means it's a year-to-date figure covering only the months elapsed so far, not a projection for the full year — a manager with a high YTD rate mid-year isn't necessarily heading for that same rate by December.
